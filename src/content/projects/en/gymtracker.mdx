problem: Most workout apps are slow to log in between sets, push subscriptions, or lose your workout when the app reloads. I wanted something fast, honest and reliable that I would actually use myself.
features:
  - Focus view with one exercise at a time, swipe between exercises and a rest timer that starts when you tick a set
  - The in-progress workout is saved continuously, so a refresh, crash or dead signal never loses it
  - Templates, 12 starter programs and repeating a past workout
  - Progress per exercise with records, trend charts and a clearly labelled estimated 1RM
  - Library of ~770 exercises with start/end photo guides
  - Per-exercise progressive overload with rep ranges
  - Dark/light mode plus custom themes with a built-in contrast check
  - Installable as a PWA, Android app in progress with Capacitor
challenges:
  - Keeping a live workout draft in Firestore safe offline without the UI ever hanging on a pending write
  - Finishing a workout as one atomic batch, so a session is never saved twice or half
  - Strict Firestore rules that validate the shape of every document, covered by emulator tests
learned:
  - Designing data around how the app is really used, not around the screens
  - Testing security rules and integration flows against the Firebase Emulator Suite
  - Shipping in small, planned iterations and getting feedback from friends who use the app
